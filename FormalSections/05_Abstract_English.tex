\noindent\textbf{Title:} Myora: Development of an AI-Powered Mobile Health and Nutrition Management Platform

\noindent\textbf{Author:} \authorname

\noindent\textbf{Supervisor:} \supname

\noindent\textbf{Department:} Software Engineering, OSTİM Technical University

\noindent\textbf{Date:} February 2026

\vspace{0.5cm}

The need for digital health solutions that aggregate individuals' health data on a single platform and deliver personalized recommendations is growing rapidly. In this project, \textbf{Myora}, a mobile-first health management platform that analyzes multi-source health data with artificial intelligence and provides user-specific nutrition, exercise, sleep, and supplement recommendations, has been developed.

Myora consolidates wearable device data (heart rate, steps, sleep, SpO$_2$, HRV), blood test results, health documents, food photographs, medical photographs, and voice diary entries into a unified AI hub. The platform leverages the OpenAI GPT-4o large language model to perform calorie and macronutrient analysis from food photos, blood marker interpretation from OCR-extracted lab texts, preliminary assessment from medical photographs, and sentiment analysis from voice recordings.

The system is built on a four-layer architecture: a React~18.3-based single-page application (SPA) client layer, an Express~5 RESTful API server layer, a PostgreSQL database layer, and a Capacitor~7 mobile layer. The database schema, defined in a type-safe manner using Drizzle ORM, comprises more than 17 tables and their interrelationships. A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture has been integrated to provide a science-backed AI health advisor chatbot.

Within the project scope, 13 core modules (AI health profile, RAG chatbot, blood test analysis, food photo analysis, supplement store, family tracking, community, wearable device integration, medical photo analysis, voice diary, intermittent fasting, goals and achievements, smart notifications) have been developed and over 80 RESTful API endpoints have been implemented.
